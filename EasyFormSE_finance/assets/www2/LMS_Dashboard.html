Electrical safety involves practices and procedures designed to prevent accidents, injuries, and fatalities from shock, burns, and fires. Key measures include using, inspecting, and maintaining equipment properly, utilizing insulation and grounding, and wearing appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) like insulated gloves and boots.
