Privacy Notice

Purpose of the Notice:

Altius is committed to ensuring the safety and security of individuals.

To fulfil this commitment, Altius collects limited personal data from individuals for the purposes of user onboarding on MWFM application, driver safety excellence and providing Permit-to-Work (PTW).

Your personal data will be used solely for the purposes mentioned in this privacy.

To enable the provision of these services, Altius, as a Data Fiduciary, and its authorized service providers (if applicable) or third parties, law enforcement agencies or legal and regulatory bodies in limited situations, will process personal data for the purposes described below.

What data? Why?
  • User & Device Data
    • First name
    • Last name
    • Mobile number
    • IP address of the mobile device
    • IMEI number
    • MAC address of the mobile device
  • Operational & Compliance Data
    • Site geo-coordinates
    • Diesel consumption records
    • Energy readings
    • Permit-to-Work records
  • Vehicle Data
    • Vehicle number
    • RC validity details
    • Driving license
    • Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate
    • Vehicle inspection images (two-wheeler/four-wheeler from all angles)
  • Personal & Safety Data
    • Educational qualifications
    • Work-related certifications
    • Images of field staff with mandatory safety equipment
    • Safety passport details (post going live)
  • Physical tower maintenance/Uptime maintenance/Preventive maintenance/Corrective maintenance performed by Site Engineers
  • Equipment and physical site verification
  • Safety governance checklist (to ensure vendor-provided safety equipment, vehicle compliance, and physical verification)
  • Diesel filling activities (performed either daily or monthly based on site requirements)
  • Geo-location tracking (to validate field staff presence at assigned sites)
  • Remote verification of field technicians upon request by Circle In-Charge / Heads
