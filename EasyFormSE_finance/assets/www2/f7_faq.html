FAQ
  • How any flow is working with Easy Form ?
  • Can I fill any checksheet form offline ?

    Yes, you can fill any type of checksheet form online as well as offline also.

  • How can I understand major entities of Easy Form ?
    Flow A flow is group of tasks.
    Task A task is group of checksheets.
    Checksheet A checksheet is group of steps.
    Step A step is group of controls.
    Control A control is a input field which is filled out from end user.
  • List out controls available in Easy Form ?

    There are number of controls available in Easy Form such as text box, radio button, dropdown, numeric box, label, check box, camera, calendar, signature, range validator, calculator, user latlong button, MSL calcualtor, distance calculator, search control, single search control, invoice calculator, collapsible, scanner, browse control, pdf viewer, video etc.

  • How to change my profile picture ?

    Open Setting go to Change Photo, here you will have two option (1) BROWSE (2) CAPTURE. You can choose any of them to save your profile picture.

  • Can I search my site from site list ?

    Yes you can, In site list a search icon is placed on top of the screen from where you can search your site by its siteid.

  • Can I check my site progress ?

    Yes you can, Once you complete any checksheet, color of your checksheet(see at the end of checksheet in checklist in blue colored) will be converted into green. You can also check individual checksheet progress by clicking PROGRESS button placed on bottom left side in a form.

  • How to know my site data has been uploaded to the server or not ?

    In site list there is one tab named Upload. It will list out sites which are still not uploaded. If you found "No RECORD FOUND !", that means all of your data has been uploaded to the server successfully.

  • What is "Skip Download" in setting menu ?

    Check it if you want to know which data are currently being downloading after successfull login, unchecked it if you just want to skip downloading screen.
